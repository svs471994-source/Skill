================================================================================
                       EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN SUMMARY
================================================================================

Experimental Parameter          Details
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Crop Species                    Oryza sativa L. (IR-64, salt-susceptible)
Experimental Phases             2 (In vitro petri-plate + Greenhouse)
Number of Isolates Tested       6 (IS-01 through IS-06)
Number of Consortia Tested      5 (C1-3, C1-5, C2-3, C2-5, C4-5)

In Vitro Conditions:
  Temperature                   25 ± 2 °C
  Photoperiod                   12 hours light/dark
  Salinity Levels               0, 50, 100, 150 mM NaCl
  Observation Period            7 days
  Replicates                    3

Greenhouse Conditions:
  Temperature                   28 ± 2 °C
  Relative Humidity             60–70%
  Photoperiod                   Natural (seasonal)
  Potting Medium                Sterilized soil:coco-peat (1:2)
  Pot Size                      15 cm × 18 cm (2.5 kg substrate)
  Salt Stress Duration          30 days (from 15 DAS)
  Total Experiment              45 days after sowing
  Replicates                    3
  Design                        Completely Randomized Design (CRD)

Biochemical Endpoints Measured  5
  1. Chlorophyll content (DMSO extraction, A₆₆₃/₆₄₅)
  2. Phenolic compounds (Folin–Ciocalteu, A₇₆₅)
  3. Soluble protein (Lowry method, A₆₆₀)
  4. Soluble carbohydrates (Anthrone assay, A₆₂₀)
  5. Antioxidant activity (DPPH radical scavenging, A₅₁₇)

Statistical Methods Applied:
  Descriptive Statistics        Mean, SD, CV
  Univariate Analysis           7 ANOVAs + Post hoc Tukey's HSD
  Multivariate Analysis         MANOVA + Discriminant Analysis
  Interaction Analysis          Two-way ANOVA (Salinity × Inoculation)
  Effect Size Estimation        Partial eta-squared (ηₚ²)
  Variance Partitioning         Coefficient of Variation (CV)

Sample Size                     n = 3 replicates per treatment
Total Observations             432 (6 isolates + 5 consortia + 2 controls ×
                                3 salinity levels × 3 replicates × 4 stages)

Quality Assurance:
  Normality Testing             Shapiro–Wilk test (p > 0.05)
  Homogeneity of Variance       Levene's test (p > 0.05)
  Significance Level            α = 0.05
  Multiple Comparison Correction Tukey's HSD (Adjusted for family-wise error)
  FDR Adjustment (Correlations) Benjamini–Hochberg (α = 0.05)
